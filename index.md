# Pawel Szczesny <span class="text-muted fw-light">(Paweł Szczęsny)</span>

**AI psychologist.** Biologist (PhD) and psychologist by training.

I work on AI Psychology, which covers two things: what synthetic minds do, and what sustained contact with them does to human minds. Language models are not just text generators. They are configurable cognitive environments that simulate perspectives, mirror their users, steer attention and change how people make sense of themselves. I study that loop from both sides and build the tools to measure it.

## Now

My company is [Impersonato](https://impersonato.com/), where we build synthetic personas with psychological structure and use them as crash-test dummies for AI products, communication and policy, so you find out what breaks before you break a real person. My research focuses on behavioural signatures of LLMs, psychology-based steering, mirroring and role capture in vulnerable users, and *cognitive sovereignty*: the ability to keep your own thinking while working with AI.

Recent papers: [LLMs as psychotherapists](/publications/#ai-psychology) (*Ethics and Information Technology*, 2026), [Unmet Demand: a measurement model for relational capture in conversational AI](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7225059) and [A Psychotechnology in the Making](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6897938).

## Before

25 years across academia, pharma R&D, science policy and AI/biotech startups:

- **Research.** PhD at the Max Planck Institute for Developmental Biology and IBB PAS. Assistant professor and team leader at the Institute of Biochemistry and Biophysics (Polish Academy of Sciences) and the University of Warsaw. Around 50 peer-reviewed papers in bioinformatics, protein evolution, translational regulation and microbial ecology.
- **Industry.** IT expert and consultant at Roche, co-founder of Nootech, R&D lead for VR exposure therapy (Fobos, 2Eye), CEO of Cognition Shared Solutions, co-founder of Neurofusion Lab.
- **Science policy and open science.** CEO of the Open Science Foundation. Advisor to the Polish Academy of Sciences, the Polish Ministry of Science and Higher Education, the European Commission and UNESCO.

Recognition: Scientific Award of *Polityka* weekly, TEDx speaker (twice), fellowships from the Max Planck Society, the Foundation for Polish Science and the Open Society Foundations.
